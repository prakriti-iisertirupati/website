On campus and around it

Sustainability on campus

The standing work — the systems the club runs quietly through the year, not one-off events. Some are already running; others are things we are setting up. Each one is meant to outlast the batch that starts it.

How to join in

Students at IISER Tirupati

Any student can help with a survey or a drive without joining the core team. Watch the announcements or write to us and we will add you to the next round.

Staff and faculty

If your work touches soil, water, waste or biodiversity on campus and you want a set of hands collecting data, tell us. Students get real fieldwork; you get a dataset.
