About the club

Prakriti

Prakriti is the Ecology and Conservation Club of IISER Tirupati — a student club running under SAC. We work across many fronts: waste management, cartography, campus biodiversity surveys, native sapling drives, and, most recently, a farmer–researcher network that carries agricultural problems from the villages around Tirupati to scientists who can help. That last one is a new initiative; more will follow as the club grows.

Prakriti is run by students. It is not an advisory service of the institute, and nothing on this site should be read as an official recommendation from IISER Tirupati.

At the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Tirupati, a set of individuals have decided to step up with a vision of a society wherein every common citizen should be involved in conservation — actively or passively — with a hope that each individual action will result in a giant push towards a sustained future.
