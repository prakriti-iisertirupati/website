About the club

Prakriti

Prakriti is the Conservation Club of IISER Tirupati — a student club running under the Student Activity Centre. We work on two things: conservation on our own campus, and a network that carries agricultural problems from farmers around Tirupati to researchers who can answer them.

Prakriti is run by students. It is not an advisory service of the institute, and nothing on this site should be read as an official recommendation from IISER Tirupati.
