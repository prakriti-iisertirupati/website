On campus and around it

Our work

The farmer network is one half of what Prakriti does. The other half is slower and closer to home: counting what lives on campus, measuring what we throw away, and planting things that belong here.

Running now

Each of these produces a record. Records outlast batches, which is the point.

How to join in

Students at IISER Tirupati

Any student can join a survey without joining the core team. Watch the announcements or write to us and we will add you to the next round.

Staff and faculty

If your work touches soil, water, waste or biodiversity on campus and you want a set of hands collecting data, tell us. Students get real fieldwork; you get a dataset.
